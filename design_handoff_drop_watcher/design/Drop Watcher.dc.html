Drop Watcher · UI design · round 1

Every screen, every state.

Each watch state owns a whole surface treatment, not a dot: waiting is a quiet grey card with a clean-read line, on sale is a solid red field with the Book button on the card itself, can't read is black with hazard tape and minutes-blind in words, silenced is an empty outline, not checked yet is dashed. Frames are live: tap dates, cinemas, copy, silence, delete.
